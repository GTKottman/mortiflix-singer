Record your voice

You sing each line as well as you can while the tuner shows where your pitch is. Being sharp or flat is fine: the model learns your voice, and tuning comes from the music later. What matters is clear words and steady notes.

  1. Microphone.
  2. Room. Stay silent for 5 seconds.
  3. Level. Sing "ah" loud for 3 seconds.
  4. Range. Slide from your lowest comfortable note to your highest.
