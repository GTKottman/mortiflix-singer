Mortiflix Singer

Write notes and words and hear them sung, or record your own voice so the singer becomes you.

  • Editor · a piano roll where every note carries its word; render, check, export to your DAW.
  • Booth · record the lines that teach the singer your voice, with a live tuner. You don't need to sing in tune.
  • Training · build your voice from your recordings.
